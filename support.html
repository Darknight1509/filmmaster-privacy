常见问题

测光结果和别的测光表不一样？

本 App 是拿手机摄像头的实时画面做反射式测光，再按你选定的胶片感光度解算光圈、快门。结果会受镜头前有没有遮光、画面里亮暗比例影响，和入射式测光表本来就存在系统性差异。建议在实拍前用固定场景对一次，找到属于你自己机身和冲洗流程的偏差值。

胶片和相机数据是从哪来的？

胶片参数取自各厂商公开的官方数据表（感光度、宽容度、倒易律失效区间、推荐冲洗工艺等），相机部分参考官方说明书与公开发行的技术资料。若你发现某条数据与你的实拍经验不符，欢迎来信指出。

App 需要联网吗？会上传我的照片吗？

测光、算景深、查资料都不需要联网，App 自身不发起任何网络请求，也不集成任何第三方 SDK。测光画面只在设备内存中实时处理，拍下的照片只写入你指定的相册，不会离开这台手机。

唯一的联网行为是「iCloud 同步」（默认开启）：胶卷记录、取景记录、胶片范围预览会存进你自己的 iCloud 私有库，我们不接触这些数据。不想同步，在「设置 → iCloud 同步」里关掉即可，其余功能照常。详见隐私政策。

「Pro 终身解锁」包含什么？怎么恢复？

Pro 为一次性买断，非订阅，解锁全部高级功能。换机或重装后，在 App 内的解锁页点「恢复购买」即可找回，同一个 Apple ID 无需重复付费。若恢复失败，先确认设备已登录购买时用的 Apple ID，再重试。

系统相机拍的照片存在哪里？

「系统相机」和「反转片预览」拍下的照片会存进系统相册，同时保留一份备份在 App 沙盒的 Documents 目录。你可以打开「文件」App，在「我的 iPhone → 胶片全能助手」下查看、导出或自行删除。卸载 App 时这些备份会一并移除。

为什么拒绝相机权限后有些功能用不了？

实时测光、系统相机和反转片预览都依赖摄像头画面，这是功能本身的需要。若你拒绝授权，仅这几项不可用，胶片库、景深计算、滤镜模拟等其余部分仍可正常使用。可在「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 相机」里随时重新打开。

数据可以自己更正吗？

可以。App 内的胶片与相机资料均支持本地查看与调整，改动留在本机；若开着 iCloud 同步，记录会一并存进你自己的 iCloud，我们不接触。

Frequently asked questions

Why do the readings differ from my other meter?

The app meters reflected light from the camera's live view and solves aperture and shutter for the film speed you selected. The result depends on what is in front of the lens and on the balance of bright and dark areas in the frame, so a systematic difference from an incident meter is expected. Before a shoot, compare them on a fixed scene and find the offset that belongs to your body and your development.

Where does the film and camera data come from?

Film parameters come from manufacturers' published data sheets (speed, latitude, reciprocity failure, recommended processing). Camera information is based on official manuals and public technical documentation. If something does not match your own experience, tell us.

Does the app need the internet? Does it upload my photos?

Metering, depth of field and the lookup tables all work offline. The app makes no network requests of its own and bundles no third-party SDKs. Live metering frames are processed in memory only, and photos you take are written to the album you choose and never leave the phone.

The one exception is iCloud sync (on by default): film rolls, viewfinder readings and film range previews are stored in your own private iCloud database, which we cannot read. Turn it off under Settings → iCloud Sync and everything else keeps working. See the privacy policy.

What does the Pro unlock include, and how do I restore it?

Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, and unlocks every advanced feature. After changing phones or reinstalling, tap "Restore Purchases" on the unlock screen; the same Apple ID is never charged twice. If a restore fails, confirm the device is signed in with the Apple ID used for the purchase and try again.

Where are the system-camera photos saved?

Photos taken with the system camera and the slide preview are saved to your photo library, with a copy kept in the app's sandbox Documents folder. Open the Files app under "On My iPhone → Film Camera Master" to view, export or delete them. Deleting the app removes these copies as well.

Why do some features stop working if I deny camera access?

Live metering, the system camera and the slide preview all need the camera feed; that is what the features are. Denying access disables only those, while the film library, depth-of-field calculation and filter simulation keep working. You can grant access again in Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera.

Can I correct the data myself?

Yes. Film and camera entries can be viewed and adjusted in the app, and your edits stay on this device. With iCloud sync on, your records are also stored in your own iCloud, which we cannot read.
